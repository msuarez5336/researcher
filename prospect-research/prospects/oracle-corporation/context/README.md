# What goes in this folder

Everything you have exchanged with this prospect. Emails are enough.

- Saved emails: `.eml` files (drag a message out of your mail app) or an `.mbox` export.
- Pasted email threads or your own notes: `.txt` or `.md`. Put the date in the file name or
  at the top, e.g. `2026-11-03-intro-call-notes.md`.
- Documents the client sent: `.pdf`, `.txt`, `.md`.

Not supported: Outlook `.msg` (save as `.eml` or paste the text).

This folder is ignored by git, so its contents stay on your machine. An empty folder is fine:
the research then relies on public sources only.
