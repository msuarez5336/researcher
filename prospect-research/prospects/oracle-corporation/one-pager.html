Openers

  1. Your 10-K says AI may keep reducing headcount and that restructuring risks skill shortages. How are you deciding which capabilities to protect?
  2. You added about $700m to the plan after August. What does the next wave mean for the US workforce, already down from 58,000 to 49,000?
  3. Five data-centre campuses for OpenAI need people Oracle doesn't employ today. Where is that talent coming from?

Ask in the meeting

  • Who owns talent strategy and total rewards, and who do they report to?
  • Do you use outside advisers for workforce planning or broad-based pay today?
  • How are pay ranges set for the new roles, and how do you handle state pay-transparency rules?
  • Who would champion outside help, and what would success look like in a year?
