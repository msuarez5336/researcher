# SQ1 notes (as of 2026-11-03)

## Answer
Only two hands-on testers were reachable with method described: Bob Vila (Oct 2026, 2+ dozen trees tested) and Yahoo Shopping (12 trees, home tests, Sep 2026). They agree on few models. Recurring 7.5-9 ft candidates: Home Depot Grand Duchess (Yahoo #1, Taste of Home positive), National Tree Co. Downswept Douglas Fir (both), King of Christmas Fraser Fir / flocked Queen Slim (both), Balsam Hill (Bob Vila #1), Puleo International (value in both). Realism is driven by PE tip content (PE outer + PVC inner is the common premium design); durability evidence is thin and mostly the unlit-tree argument for light failure.

## Findings
- Bob Vila picks: Balsam Hill Classic Blue Spruce best overall (SQ1-1); NTC Downswept Douglas Fir most realistic (2); King of Christmas King Fraser Fir best prelit (3); Puleo Aspen Fir best value (4); Puleo Slim Balsam Fir best slim, 9 ft (5); Balsam Hill Fraser Fir Flip easiest setup (6); BCP flocked 9 ft (7); NTC Dunhill unlit (8). [E] No warranty info given.
- Yahoo: Grand Duchess #1 (10), Puleo Teton Pine value, 5-yr warranty (11, 12), NTC Douglas Fir best for decorating (13), KoC Queen Flock Slim best flocked, sheds (15). [E]
- Taste of Home month-long test of Grand Duchess: little debris, lights fine (16). [E]
- PE tips = realism; PE/PVC mix common in nicer trees (17, 18). [O]
- Review Hub (own scoring, weaker): Yorkshire Fir, Vermont White Spruce, Grand Duchess (19-21). [O]
- Durability: Yahoo tester notes pre-lit lights failed in past, favors unlit (14) [O]. Flocked trees shed (7, 15) [E]. No tester measured multi-season light failure.
