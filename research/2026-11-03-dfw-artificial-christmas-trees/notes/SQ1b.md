## Findings
- Taste of Home and Reviewed both pick Balsam Hill as top realism; Bob Vila also picks Balsam Hill Classic Blue Spruce best overall [E] (1,11).
- Taste of Home matches Yahoo on Grand Duchess being strong (7.5 ft, month test) [E] (4). Yahoo put it #1; ToH calls it Best Smart, not overall.
- CNN and Reviewed both back NTC Downswept Douglas Fir, agreeing with Bob Vila "most realistic" and Yahoo "best for decorating" [E/O] (9,13).
- KoC Queen Flock: ToH best flocked, agrees with Yahoo; but a stand defect and lean were noted [E] (7,8).
- Complaints: Blue Spruce wobbly, hard to fluff, weak under heavy ornaments (2); Fraser Fir droops with heavy ornament (6).
- Search snippets: Bob Vila rated Grand Duchess 4.1/5 with frequent stock-outs (unverified, not cached).
