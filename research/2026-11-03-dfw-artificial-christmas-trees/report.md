## Quality: what makes a tree look real and last

- **Needle material.** [O] (SQ1-17, SQ1-18) ✓
  - PE needles are molded from real branches, and the more PE a tree has, the more realistic
    it tends to look.
  - Many nicer trees now use PE on the outside and cheaper PVC inside.
  - Both points are designer and editor opinion, not measurements.
- **Pre-lit vs unlit.** A Yahoo tester recommends unlit trees after past pre-lit light
  failures, because a dead built-in strand can mean replacing the tree. [O] (SQ1-14) ✓
  - No tester measured how often lights fail over several seasons.
  - A warranty that covers the lights (Balsam Hill's 3 years) matters more on a pre-lit tree.
    (SQ1b-6) ✓
- **Flocked trees shed.** Two testers reported faux-snow shedding or a messy setup. [E] (SQ1-7,
  SQ1-15) ✓

## Outdated or weak material

- **Holiday Warehouse (Plano)** comes from a 2014 D Magazine guide and is probably closed (see
  above).
- **Reviewed** is dated 2025-11-11 and tested 6.5–7.5 ft trees, so it covers only the bottom of
  your size range.
- **Bob Vila's review page** is labelled "AI assisted, editor reviewed" and carries "ON SALE NOW"
  affiliate links.
  - Its picks are still listed with test details (tip counts, bump tests), but give it less
    weight than a fully hands-on test.
  - Bob Vila's deal pages (SQ2, SQ2b) are affiliate roundups. Their prices are dated snapshots,
    not independent price checks.
- **The tariff figures** (10–15%) describe 2025. No 2026 update was found.

## Method and limitations

- **Workers.** Six `researcher` agents in two waves:
  - Wave 1: SQ1 quality, SQ2 prices, SQ3 DFW stores, 15 calls each.
  - Wave 2: SQ1b, SQ2b, SQ3b, 12 calls each, aimed at the gaps wave 1 left.
- **Evidence.** 74 items. `check_quotes.py`: 74 of 74 quotes found verbatim in the cached pages.
- **Claim check.** Two fresh-context `claim-verifier` agents checked 10 load-bearing claims
  (`claims/`, `verdicts/`).
  - Result: 5 supported, 5 partial, 0 unsupported, contradicted or unverifiable. Every partial
    claim was narrowed in this report.
  - What was narrowed:
    - The NTC tree's lit and unlit versions are now kept separate.
    - CNN's use is two past seasons, not three.
    - Balsam Hill is no longer called every tester's realism pick.
    - Lowe's "up to 40%" is shown as a headline: the listed trees were about 9–16% off.
    - The Black Friday 2025 low end is about $95 (slim trees).
    - The $99.99 deal was before Christmas.
    - King of Christmas "was" prices and its "up to 70%" headline are labelled as such.
