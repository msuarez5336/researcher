Swing Options Backtest Spec

Rules for testing a 3–10 day directional options swing strategy on liquid US underlyings. Every rule can be coded and every result can be falsified. Checked against vendor docs, fee schedules and the overfitting literature as of 6 Nov 2026.

DTE ruler and spec linter

The original spec had entry at 30 DTE, an exit at DTE < 21, and a hold of up to 15 days, and those three can't all hold. Change the numbers to see whether a variant is consistent before you spend money on data.

Fill cost per leg

Buy = bid + k·(ask − bid). Sell = ask − k·(ask − bid). Applied per leg, and the cost is charged on entry and exit. k = 0.5 is mid.

The v2 spec

changed means corrected from v1. new means added in v2. Untagged rules are kept as written.

1

Hypothesis and universe

  • SPY, QQQ, and S&P 500 members as of each historical date whose options trade more than 10,000 contracts a day. point-in-time
  • One catalyst and one structure per test, long premium only: a 0.60–0.70Δ single leg or a 0.50/0.25Δ debit vertical. Credit spreads are a separate hypothesis. changed
  • Max hold 10 trading days. one number
  • Write these down before running: pass/fail gates, position sizing (risk a fixed % of equity at the debit), max concurrent positions, max per underlying, and a shares-only baseline on the same signal. new
2

Data

  • Point-in-time bid/ask (NBBO) chains, never last trade. Record the vendor's snapshot time and execute at it. EOD data is a pre-close snapshot: ORATS ≈ 15:46 ET, Cboe 15:45 ET and close. changed
  • IV and Greeks: use the vendor's if supplied. Otherwise compute them with one American model (e.g. Bjerksund-Stensland), with a named rate curve and dividends. Never mix the two.
  • Earnings dates, ex-dividend dates and amounts, delisted tickers, historical index membership. Unadjusted prices for strike and Δ maths. Exclude adjusted (non-standard) contracts. new
  • Open interest: the latest value dated before entry (at best the prior day). Some feeds lag a further day. new
3

Contract selection

  • Expiry 35–60 DTE, nearest 45. was 30–60
  • Strike by Δ. Reject the trade if no strike sits in the band.
  • Bid-ask spread ≤ 5–8% of mid and prior-day OI ≥ 500, on every leg.
4

Entry, management, exit

  • Signal on the underlying at bar t. Execute at the next snapshot, or at a fixed intraday time only if you have intraday data. changed
  • When several exits fire, the higher one wins: Event > Stop > Time > Profit. new
  • Event: close 1 trading day before earnings. Stop: underlying invalidation, with a −50% option-value backstop. A gap fills at the next quote. Time: 10 days, or DTE < 21. Profit: 50% of max on spreads, +35% on single legs. split by structure
  • Close any short call that is ITM before ex-dividend when its extrinsic value (≈ the same-strike put) is below the dividend. The expiration-Friday pin rule is cut because it never fires above a 21-DTE floor. changed
5

Simulation and validation

  • Fills per leg at k = 0.70, stress at k = 1.0. Costs about $0.70 per contract per side ($0.65 commission + ~$0.05 fees). contradiction fixed
  • Rolling walk-forward (3y train / 1y test) plus an untouched final holdout, with the regimes named. was a 60/40 split
  • Grid: DTE {35, 45, 55} × Δ {0.40, 0.50, 0.60} × target {25, 35, 50%}. Accept a plateau, never a single peak.
  • Log every configuration. Report the probability of backtest overfitting (PBO, via CSCV) or a deflated Sharpe ratio. new
  • Pass gates (OOS, k = 1.0): expectancy > 0 · profit factor ≥ 1.3 · ≥ 100 trades (30 floor) · positive in ≥ 2 of 3 regimes · beats the shares baseline · drawdown within the stated limit. new

    Tickers for long options

    Verdict: no liquid ticker shows favourable odds for buying options on its own. On every ticker with a free implied-vol series, a 10-day ATM straddle priced at implied lost money before costs. Compression ("squeeze") was not followed by outsized ranges on any of the 24 candidates. A long-strangle test must beat a negative baseline with an entry filter.

    10-day ATM straddle priced at implied, mean gross return

    Nasdaq daily closes 2016–2026, priced at each ticker's Cboe 30-day vol index (VIX, RVX, VXN, VXEWZ, VXAZN, VXGOG, VXAPL). Gross of spreads and commissions. All are significant except AAPL (t = −0.4). Entering only when implied < 20-day realised cut the loss on every ticker but never made it positive.

    Long-strangle addendum to the spec

    S
    • Long call + long put, 0.25–0.30Δ each, 35–60 DTE, exit by 21 DTE or day 10. Run an ATM straddle on the same dates as a control.
    • Entry filter: implied move (straddle mid ÷ spot, scaled to the hold) < trailing 10-day realised move. A squeeze can only be an extra filter, tested against this one.
    • Exits: +40% / −40% of the total debit, the time exit, and the day before earnings. All are grid parameters.
    • Must beat random-date entries and trigger-only entries at k = 1.0 on both legs, plus the v2 gates. Report the median trade too.
    • Variant S-E (earnings): ~5–14 DTE straddle bought at the close before the print and sold after, only when implied < historical earnings move. A vendor study found +36.7% per trade in that quintile and −18.5% overall. It is in-sample and unreplicated.

    When opportunities open

    Each episodic window was tested by buying options at the time the window opened, pricing them at the Cboe implied vol, repricing them at exit, and comparing against the same option bought on random days. The study covers 2016–2026, split in half to check whether results held.

    Verdict: no window beat random entries in a way that chance can't explain. The clearest results are negative. Buying the bounce after a big index down-day lost 21 points against random days, and implied vol falls 1–1.6 points in the 10 days after shocks, which drags on any call or put bought for a rebound.

    The lead is probably a false positive: 2 passes out of ~81 eligible tests is about the chance rate. Its pricing also used a stand-in IV for most stocks that can't show the post-shock IV drop. It goes forward only as a confirmation test on real option-chain data. The default structure after any shock is a debit spread. Not tested: CPI and payrolls (no date source) and earnings (excluded).
