What to do when each signal fires

In the order the signal can appear, from the prior evening to the close.

Prior close → next session

Day after a big stock-specific move

Watch
Trigger
Single stock moves ≥ 2.5σ of its 60-day range, the market doesn't (|SPY| < 1σ), volume ≥ 2× its 20-day average, close in the top or bottom 20% of the day's range, VIX not in its top third. No earnings that day or the day before (H-MOM1).
Buy
ATM call (up move) or put (down move), 7–14 DTE. Enter at the move-day close, or at the next open if you missed it.
Exit
Next day's close.
Evidence
2021–26: next day +2.9% (t 3.6, n 27), of which +2.1% open→close. Options: close entry +43 pts, open entry +29 pts vs random days.

It reversed in 2016–21 (−7 to −12 pts), so it depends on the regime. The open entry keeps about two-thirds of the edge, because most of the continuation comes during the session, not overnight. IV was a proxy.

09:30 (or 10:30)

Gap fill

Watch
Trigger
Opening gap at least 1× the standard deviation of the last 60 gaps (around 0.4–0.5% on SPY lately).
Buy
0DTE ATM option against the gap (call after a gap down, put after a gap up), or an ATM/0.25Δ debit spread.
Exit
First hourly mark where the trade is up after costs. Otherwise out by 15:30.
Evidence
SPY: +8 to +10 pts average excess over 40 variants; best +16.7 pts (t 1.6–2.05). QQQ: +3 to +9 pts. In-sample same sign, smaller.

Not significant on its own. There's no academic study of gap fill, only this run's data (87 out-of-sample gap days). Example: 2026-10-30, SPY gapped −1.0% and kept falling, and the fill call lost 98%.

09:30

Gap-and-go on SPY

Avoid
Trigger
Same gap as above.
Don't
Buy a 0DTE call or put in the gap's direction on SPY.
Evidence
SPY: −11 to −15 pts average excess; 14 of 40 variants at t ≤ −2. QQQ: −2 to −10 pts.

This is the most consistent negative result among the directional setups. On CPI/payroll days the gap rules show nothing either way.

10:30

First-hour continuation, QQQ

Watch
Trigger
QQQ's 09:30→10:30 move is at least its 60-day median first-hour move.
Buy
0DTE ATM in the move's direction at 10:30, or an ATM/0.25Δ debit spread.
Exit
Hold to 15:30. Early exits gave back most of the edge here.
Evidence
Out-of-sample +11 pts (t 1.1, n 175); in-sample +40 pts (t 2.9).

Fading the first hour has no edge, and SPY continuation is ≈ 0. Opening-range-breakout papers look strong gross of costs, but an independent net-of-cost replication found ≈ 0.

14:30, FOMC days only

Fade the statement into the press conference

Watch
Trigger
FOMC statement at 14:00. Note the direction of the 13:30→14:30 move.
Buy
0DTE ATM option against that move at 14:30 (or an ATM/0.25Δ debit spread).
Exit
15:30.
Evidence
SPY out-of-sample +47 pts, but only 13 meetings; in-sample −5. Powell's press conferences carry more than 3× the volatility of his predecessors' and tend to reverse the statement move (IJCB 2026).

Too few events to judge. Hourly bars can't separate 14:00 from 14:30; this needs minute data.

15:30

Last half-hour momentum

No edge
Trigger
Direction of the move from the prior close to 10:30 (intraday momentum).
Result
Excess ≈ 0 against a random side. Absolute loss of about 25–43% per trade at k = 0.70.

The underlying effect is real in long samples but too small to beat 30 minutes of 0DTE decay plus the widest spreads of the day.

Rules that apply to every setup

Pick the structure for the signal's strength

1DTE ATM decays least over a few hours. 0.30Δ OTM 0DTE is the most extreme in both directions. The debit spread narrows outcomes but had the worst average, because it pays the spread twice. No structure turned a weak signal into an edge.

Costs are the main toll

ATM SPX 0DTE options average about 9% of premium in effective spread (2004–21). Spreads are widest at the open and into the close. Use limit orders and never assume a fill at mid.

Exits

Be out by 15:30–15:45; never carry a 0DTE to settlement. A first-profit exit is cost-sensitive: a +1.8% gross gain can be 0% net, so the rule doesn't fire. Pick one exit rule per setup in advance.

Size

Risk a fixed small debit per trade. Retail long 0DTE positions lost 53–61% on average in the Beckmeyer–Branger–Gayda data (as summarised).

No directional edge found; skip these
  • Fading the first hour (SPY or QQQ).
  • Trading in the CPI or payroll direction after the 08:30 release: excess between −5 and +6 pts, all t < 1.
  • Monthly OpEx Fridays as a long-option day. Dealers stay net long gamma, and moves are smaller than implied.
  • Single-stock "0DTE": only about nine names have Monday/Wednesday expiries since 2026-01-26. There are no daily expiries.
