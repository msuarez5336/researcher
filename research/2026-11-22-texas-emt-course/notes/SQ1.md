# SQ1 notes (as of 2026-11-22)

*Answer:* No official source I read mentions citizenship, lawful presence or SSN for Texas EMT certification or NREMT. Stated requirements: 18+, HS diploma/GED, DSHS-approved course, jurisprudence exam, NREMT exam, application and fee, FBI fingerprint check, finish within 2 years of course. Immigration and SSN treatment is unresolved from official sources.

## 1 Citizenship/immigration. No evidence found in DSHS page or 25 TAC 157.33 (read originals, cached). Federal 8 USC 1621 licence rule not checked for EMS. A search snippet (not cached, unverified) says Texas BHEC cites 8 USC 1621 and TDLR uses lawful-presence proof; those are other agencies, not DSHS EMS. DACA/visa/undocumented: no evidence found. Texas DACA work-permit litigation is reported by Texas Tribune 2025 (snippet only). Recommend asking EMSCert@dshs.texas.gov, 512-834-6734.
## 2 SSN. No evidence found for DSHS EMS or NREMT. Snippets: Massachusetts accepts an NREMT-assigned pseudo-SSN (mass.gov OEMS FAQ, snippet only, unverified); a DSHS volunteer sign-off form asks for EMS ID or SSN (Family Code ch. 232), snippet only. ITIN/affidavit for Texas EMS: not found.
## 3 Age. 18 for certification (E SQ1-1, SQ1-2; originals read). Course minimum age: no evidence found; DSHS search summary cites 157.33 re minors waiting until 18 (not verified).
## 4 Education. HS diploma from TEA-accredited or other-state accredited school, or GED; home school OK; foreign education needs transcript evaluation attesting equivalency; ECA volunteers exempt (SQ1-2; original read).
## 5 Criminal history. FBI fingerprints via IdentoGO required (SQ1-3). Prescreening before enrolling exists, $50 plus DPS fingerprint fee (SQ1-10, 11). Denial is case-by-case for offenses related to EMS duties (SQ1-12); I did not read the full list of 157.37(e)(5) automatic bars. NREMT handbook mentions felony conviction (Candidate Responsibilities page, line not read in context; not recorded).
## 6 Steps. Approved course (DSHS course number starts with 6), NREMT exam (DSHS says take before applying; SQ1-6), jurisprudence exam (SQ1-4; DSHS page omits it), apply online, fingerprints, processing up to 4 weeks, 2-year limit (SQ1-5). NREMT skills verification requirements not recorded: not read.
## 7 Fees. DSHS page: EMT $64 (read in cache but not quoted as table); rule text: $60 (SQ1-7). NREMT $104 (SQ1-9). Fingerprint fee amount not found. Prescreen $50.
