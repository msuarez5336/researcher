# SQ3 notes (as of 2026-11-22)
## Answer
Public EMT-Basic courses range from about $871 (Alvin, in-district, tuition+fees) to $4,181 (TEEX, published total). Community college in-district rates are cheapest paid route. Funded route: DSHS SB 8 scholarship via an EMS sponsor (one-year ambulance commitment, repayment if unmet) is known only from search snippets (unverified).
