# SQ2 notes (as of 2026-11-22)

## Answer
Texas rule text (Cornell snapshot) requires only 150 total clock hours of classroom, lab, clinical and field instruction, including ED and licensed-EMS supervised experience, with no separate clinical minimum (SQ2-1). A DSHS March 2026 slide shows a proposal to move to 192 h with 48 clinical/field (SQ2-2, snippet only, unverified). RC Health's 72 clinical hours (48 EMS + 24 ED) is a real, quantified figure, but its DFW sites are not named; Dallas College and UT Dallas are the only ones naming local sites or fuller structure.

## Baseline
- Texas: min 150 clock hours total for EMT [E] SQ2-1. Proposed 192 (144 + 48 clinical/field) [E, unverified] SQ2-2. NHTSA National EMS Education Standards baseline: not fetched (budget).

## Structural differences
- Only RC (72 h) and UT Dallas (48 h) publish clinical hours; Dallas EMT, TCC, NEMSA, Texas EMS School do not on fetched pages.
- Only UT Dallas and Dallas College name local sites; RC's "major hospitals" claim is generic [O].
- RC hybrid has fewer clinical hours (24) than its traditional (72) page.
- Dallas College 272 h is far above 150 h minimum; community colleges run semester-length.

## Conflicts
